🔄 Research Pipeline

A research task can eventually move through several stages.

1. Question

Define the question that needs to be investigated.

2. Planning

Break the question into smaller research objectives.

3. Investigation

Gather relevant information for the research task.

4. Organization

Organize useful information and findings.

5. Analysis

Compare and examine the collected information.

6. Findings

Produce useful conclusions from the research.

7. Memory

Store useful research information for later retrieval.

8. Complete

Mark the research task as completed when appropriate.
